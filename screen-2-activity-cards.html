Design Notes — Activity Cards

  • Expanded cards show full details: icon, name, category, quarter, budget, duration, source, status badge, rationale, and AI confidence.
  • AI-generated cards have amber gradient background, amber border, sparkle icon, confidence percentage, and a rationale section.
  • Standard cards use the mission-type colour as top accent bar and a clean white background.
  • Inline timeline bars are compact versions showing only name, cost hint, and status — full detail on hover/click.
  • Drag handles appear on hover for repositioning on the timeline.
  • Overflow menus provide Edit, Duplicate, Move, and Delete actions.
  • Click-to-open triggers the detail side drawer (see Screen 7 for edit flow).
