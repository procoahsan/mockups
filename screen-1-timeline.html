Design Notes — Timeline Layout

  • Sticky headers: Year and quarter rows stay fixed during vertical and horizontal scroll.
  • Mission labels: Left column is sticky and always visible even when scrolling horizontally.
  • Quarterly grid: 20 cells representing Q1–Q4 across 5 years, with major separators at year boundaries.
  • Today marker: Red vertical line shows the current date position on the timeline.
  • Milestones: Diamond markers indicate key checkpoints (Year 1 review, mid-plan checkpoint).
  • Budget heatmap: Bottom row shows quarterly budget intensity for at-a-glance spending patterns.
  • Zoom controls: Users can zoom the timeline from 60% to 150% to manage wide or narrow views.
  • Activity bars: Positioned and sized based on quarter offset and span, with colour coding by mission type.
  • AI-generated items: Visually distinguished with amber/gold border and sparkle icon.
