Design Notes — Yearly Kanban Timeline

  • Yearly Columns: The timeline is divided into 5 columns, one for each year in the roadmap. Quarters have been removed.
  • Drag and Drop: Users can drag activity blocks and drop them into different year columns to easily reschedule them.
  • Activity Cards: Each block shows the mission name, category, status, and AI confidence if applicable.
  • Empty States: If a year is empty, a dashed "Drop activity here" placeholder naturally guides the user.
