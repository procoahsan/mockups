Design Notes

  • #1 Baseline label: "Ref" renamed to "Baseline" with a tooltip explaining it's the most recent PVS Evaluation year used as the starting reference.
  • #2 Confidence gradient: Each year column has a tinted header — green (high certainty) fading to amber/red (speculative) — communicating that planning reliability decreases over the horizon.
  • #3 Budget row: Each year column shows explicit dollar figures ($385K, $280K, etc.) inline so values are readable in print and screenshots without hover.
  • #4 Milestones: Diamond markers now have inline text labels ("Year 1 Review", "Mid-Plan Checkpoint") visible directly in the column header.
  • #5 Today marker: A red bottom-border highlights the current year column. When the roadmap is entirely in the future, it shows a "Pre-roadmap" state instead of a cramped edge marker.
  • #6 Card labels: Each card shows the activity name, category, and budget. Cards are ordered chronologically within each year column.
  • #8 Legend grouping: The legend is split into three labelled sections — Mission Types, Overlays, and Confidence — instead of a flat row.
  • #9 Collisions: Activities in the same year stack vertically as individual cards, eliminating overlap. The kanban layout handles collisions inherently.
  • #10 AI toggle: A toggle button in both the sidebar and the main toolbar lets users show/hide AI-recommended activities. Hidden AI cards fade out; the count badge stays visible.
