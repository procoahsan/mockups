Design Notes â€” Edit Flow

  • Edit Mode toggle activates/deactivates the floating toolbar and card selection highlights.
  • Selected cards get a 3px orange outline + glow to clearly indicate selection state.
  • Floating toolbar uses glassmorphism and only enables destructive actions when a card is selected.
  • Detail drawer slides from the right with full edit form. AI rationale is read-only but visible.
  • AI cards are always distinguishable via amber gradient, gold border, sparkle badge, and confidence %.
  • Ghost card (dashed border) appears in edit mode to invite users to add new activities.
  • When a CBD staff member edits an AI card, the status changes to "Edited by CBD" automatically.
