Design Notes — Executive Summary

  • Designed as a collapsible right panel on the main timeline view (toggleable via "Summary" button).
  • Progress ring uses WOAH brand orange (#FF4815) as the primary accent.
  • Annual spending bar chart uses gradient fills from brand orange to softer orange.
  • Budget breakdown includes both horizontal bars and a donut/pie chart for visual variety.
  • Cohort-level analytics show cross-country data relevant to CBD coordinators.
  • All numbers are dynamic and would update when timeline filters are applied.
